Introduction
The purpose of this document is to describe the data of the Semiconductor Manufacturing with AMHS Testbed 2022 (SMAT2022). SMAT2022 contains 16 different types of tables: 7 tables from SMT2020 and new nine tables describing AMHS in modern FABs. A summary of the purpose of tables is as follows:
Table	Description
Address	Information on all nodes of OHT rail network, and their characteristics.
ZCU	Information on
Rail	Information on all links of OHT rail network.
Bay	Information on bays (Intrabay and Interbay)
Equipment	Information on
PortType	Information on
Port	Information on 
VehicleType	Information on 
Vehicle	Information on
